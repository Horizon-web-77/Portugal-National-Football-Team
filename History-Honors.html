History of Portugal Football

The Portugal national football team was founded in 1914 and represents Portugal in international football competitions. The team is managed by the Portuguese Football Federation (FPF).

Portugal has developed into one of Europe's successful national football teams, with talented players and important achievements in international tournaments.

Over the years, Portugal has experienced unforgettable moments, including its first major European title in 2016 and victories in the UEFA Nations League.

Key Milestones

1966

World Cup Achievement

Portugal finished third in the FIFA World Cup in England.

2004

European Championship Final

Portugal hosted UEFA EURO 2004 and finished as runners-up.

2016

European Champions

Portugal defeated France 1–0 in the UEFA EURO final and won its first major international title.

2019

Nations League Winners

Portugal won the first UEFA Nations League after defeating the Netherlands.

2025

Another Nations League Title

Portugal defeated Spain on penalties to win its second Nations League title.
